# Opportunity ranking: evidence and thesis alignment plan

Study cutoff: 2026-10-10 UTC. Baseline source commit: `764f3c1` (rubric `1.3.0-evidence-consistent-opportunity`). The deployed baseline remains the older `1.2.6` release until direct Railway verification proves otherwise.

## Objective and fixed contract

The user's target is an undervalued business with defensible value, an evidenced and financeable catalyst, manageable downside, and a plausible path to substantial upside over roughly six months. The 50% upside threshold is a research hypothesis, not an observed probability or price target. Keep the eight factor weights **25/20/15/12/10/10/5/3**, the existing component denominators, exact hundredth arithmetic, the complete numerical universe, and score-descending/symbol-BINARY ordering. Missing evidence earns zero. A numerical research rank must remain distinguishable from a thesis-qualified candidate.

## Study findings

Read `HANDOFF.md`, `docs/PROJECT_MEMORY.md`, `docs/OPERATIONS_MEMORY.md`, the prior plan/report/validation artifacts, acceptance contract, acquisition, normalization, dossier, proxy, evaluator, persistence, API, UI and historical-validation code. Independent algorithm, validation and production audits cross-checked the findings.

1. The automatic catalyst model allocates its existing 50/30/20 components to observed revenue growth, backlog and a scheduled earnings window. Those inputs do not establish an economically material funded future event. The reviewed scorer can assess such events, but acquisition does not populate a reviewed catalyst dossier. A high numerical grade therefore cannot be advertised as a verified buying thesis.
2. Every `snapshot.sourceConflicts` string currently suppresses all proxy factors, even if the contradiction concerns one independent financial field. Keep the conflict and the decision block, but withhold only model components that depend on the disputed input. Unknown or unclassifiable conflicts must continue to fail closed.
3. Valuation can earn relative-multiple and yield points without an intrinsic estimate. Those points are useful research signals, not a demonstrated margin of safety. Surface explicit thesis prerequisites instead of silently promoting a high overall score into a qualified idea.
4. The previous 7,506-row same-input replay proves deterministic grade integrity, not stock-selection success. There are zero usable mature six-month observations. A current-universe backtest would introduce survivorship and future-data bias. Keep probabilities and target claims null.
5. Production has a live 7,517-listing acquisition at stage 11 but its 5 GB volume is below the app's 256 MiB physical safety reserve. The last deployment is the older rubric. Do not start a competing scan, discard the volume or force a full evaluation repair while space is inadequate.

## Wave 1 — reproduce and repair independent evidence

- Create field-scoped conflict interpretation for known typed conflict producers. Map raw SEC fields to derived snapshot dependencies (for example OCF/capex to FCF); treat ambiguous legacy strings as global blockers. Preserve every raw conflict in the evaluation and keep source eligibility false until all contradictions are resolved.
- Add adversarial and valid controls: an unrelated revenue dispute cannot erase a sourced technical component; a revenue dispute withholds revenue-dependent components; an unknown dispute suppresses all model credit; ordering and exact weights remain stable.
- Reconcile source dates, rights, scope, accounting periods and currency on the components touched by this change. Bump the rubric so persisted scores cannot silently reuse the old formula.

## Wave 2 — make the stock thesis explicit

- Make the reviewed catalyst and valuation prerequisites visible as auditable gates separate from the complete mathematical rank. A candidate must have a current security and price, positive market cap, sufficient liquidity, compatible valuation with the intended upside, a sourced material catalyst inside the horizon, funding for the catalyst, and no unresolved adverse risk. These gates must not change the eight weights, the complete ranking or invent event economics.
- Ensure the automatic backlog, historical growth and earnings-calendar signals stay labelled as research indicators. Verified 8-K/Form 4 discovery metadata remain unreviewed until their issuer identity, terms, funding and dates have been checked against primary documents.
- Audit a blind sample of the largest score/rank movers and the top 20/100 against the saved evidence. Record false positives, independent-credit recovery, missing data and unsupported instrument cases; refine only reproducible defects.

## Wave 3 — independent measurement

- Freeze identical saved inputs and the previous evaluator, replay baseline and candidate over the full universe, and report changed components, rank movement, top-set overlap, zero grades, source coverage, conflict slices and thesis-qualified count. Independently recompute every component and final weighted score.
- Run typecheck, lint, engine, runtime/API, research, SQLite and verified build/UI gates. Run strict-import identity and deterministic repeatability checks. Keep raw/private exports outside Git.
- Do not claim better realized returns from this replay. A predictive test needs historical then-listed and delisted membership, filing availability, original price/actions/dividends, executable next-session entry, six-month total-return outcomes and matched benchmarks. Compare baseline and candidate paired by date, use issuer holdout and purged chronological windows, and report sample size and uncertainty. Publish probability only if that independent gate passes.

## Wave 4 — delivery and live acceptance

- Preserve the active Railway scan and existing private state. Record current deployment, cursor, filesystem free space and backup integrity. Increase capacity only with user authorization if it changes the plan or recurring cost; otherwise keep production unchanged when the safety reserve fails.
- Commit and push reviewed changes. Deploy explicitly only when capacity and CI gates permit. Verify the deployed rubric, all saved factor/final grades and rank order, radar/report/profile/export parity, pinned pagination, source-review state and mobile rendering. Update the three current memory records with observed identities and unresolved limits.

Success means fewer demonstrated scoring/evidence errors, truthful thesis qualification, complete deterministic ranking, and an honest measured comparison. A higher investment win rate remains unproven until the point-in-time outcome gate has real data.
